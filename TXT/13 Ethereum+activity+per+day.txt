*** Ethereum Activity per day ***


- Create a new file under models: eth_activity_per_day.sql


select
date,
transaction_category,
count(*) as tx_count,
sum(value)/1e18 as sum_eth_value

from {{ ref('transactions_enriched') }}

group by 
date,
transaction_category


- Then from the terminal:

	dbt run --select eth_activity_per_day
