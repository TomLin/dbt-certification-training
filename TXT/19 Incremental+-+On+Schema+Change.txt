*** Incremental Models - On Schema Change ***



- Link to the on schema change parameter: 

	https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/incremental-models#what-if-the-columns-of-my-incremental-model-change



- Add a new field in transactions enriched.sql:


	1 as new_field
	
- Then from the terminal:


	dbt run --select transactions_enriched
	
	And see in Snowflake if the new_field appears in the table




- Then add an on_schema_change value ( fail) to transactions_enriched:

	{{ config(materialized='incremental', incremental_strategy='append', on_schema_change='sync_all_columns')}}

- Then from the terminal:


	dbt run --select transactions_enriched
	
	And see your model fail




- Finally add an on_schema_change value ( sync_all_columns) to transactions_enriched:

	{{ config(materialized='incremental', incremental_strategy='append', on_schema_change='sync_all_columns')}}



- Then from the terminal:


	dbt run --select transactions_enriched
	
	And see in Snowflake how the new column appears
