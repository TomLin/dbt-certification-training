*** Configuring sources in dbt ***


- On configuring sources, we can put an identifier to a source by editing in sources.yml:


version: 2

sources:
  - name: eth
    database: eth
    schema: eth_schema

    tables:
      - name: contracts
		    identifier: cnt
      - name: token_transfers
      - name: transactions



- Back in our terminal:

		dbt compile -s stg_contracts









- To quote a database and schema from a source:



version: 2

sources:
  - name: eth
	quoting:
		database: true
		schema: true
    database: eth
    schema: eth_schema

    tables:
      - name: contracts
		identifier: cnt
      - name: token_transfers
      - name: transactions



- Back in our terminal:

		dbt compile -s stg_contracts
