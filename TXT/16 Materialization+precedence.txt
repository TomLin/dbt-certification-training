*** Materialization precedence ***


- Under dbt_project.yml do the following modification on models key:


models:
  eth:
    +materialized: table

- Then do from the terminal:

	dbt run


- Then under stg_contracts.sql file add this line on top of the file and save:


{{ config(materialized='view')}}


- Then from the terminal:

	dbt run --select stg_contracts
