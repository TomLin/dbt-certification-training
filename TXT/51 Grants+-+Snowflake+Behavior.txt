*** Grants - Snowflake Behavior ***


- Back in Snowflake:

		CREATE ROLE TESTER;
		
		GRANT ROLE TESTER TO USER your_user_name;
		
		USE SECONDARY ROLES NONE;
		
		USE ROLE TESTER;
		

- Refresh the Database Explorer: nothing shows.


- Then:

		USE ROLE ACCOUNTADMIN;
		
		GRANT SELECT ON TABLE DBT.YOUR_DBT_SCHEMA.STABLECOIN_ACTIVITY_PER_DAY TO ROLE TESTER;
		
		USE ROLE TESTER;
		

- Refresh the Database Explorer: still nothing shows.


- Then:

		USE ROLE ACCOUNTADMIN;
		
		GRANT USAGE ON DATABASE DBT TO ROLE TESTER;
		
		GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA DBT.YOUR_DBT_SCHEMA TO ROLE TESTER;
		
		GRANT USAGE ON WAREHOUSE COMPUTE_WH TO TOLE TESTER;
		
		USE ROLE TESTER;
				


- Refresh the Database Explorer: now you see your table, schema and database


- Now reset the privileges of role TESTER:


		USE ROLE ACCOUNTADMIN;
		
		DROP ROLE TESTER;
		
		CREATE ROLE TESTER;
		
		GRANT ROLE TESTER TO USER your_user_name;
