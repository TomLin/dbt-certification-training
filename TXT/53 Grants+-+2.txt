*** Grants - 2 ***

- Link on the Grants config documentation:

		https://docs.getdbt.com/reference/resource-configs/grants
		

- Link on hooks documentation:

		https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/hooks-operations


- Back in Snowflake:

		USE ROLE ACCOUNTADMIN;
		
		CREATE ROLE BA;
		
		GRANT ROLE BA TO USER your_user_name;
		
		

- Back in dbt_project.yml file modify the models key like so ( ! don't forget to replace by your own schema ! ):


models:
  eth:
    +materialized: table
    +tags: ['eth', 'crypto']
    marts:
      +tags:
        - powerbi
        - report
      +schema: marts

      analytics:
        stablecoin_activity_per_day:
          +post-hook:
            - GRANT USAGE ON DATABASE DBT TO ROLE TESTER;
            - GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA DBT.YOUR_SCHEMA TO ROLE TESTER;
            - GRANT USAGE ON DATABASE DBT TO ROLE BA;
            - GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA DBT.YOUR_SCHEMA TO ROLE BA;
          +grants:
            select: ['BA']
			

- Back in our terminal:

		dbt run -m stablecoin_activity_per_day
		


- Now turn your grants config into ad additive config. Replace the config block on top of stablecoin_activity_per_day by (with the + sign):

{{ config(tags=['stablecoin'], grants = {'+select': ['TESTER']})}}



- Back in our terminal:

		dbt run -m stablecoin_activity_per_day
