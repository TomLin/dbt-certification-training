*** dbt init ***

- To activate the virtual environment from the command prompt in Vs Code:

	cd venv

	cd Scripts

	activate


- To come back to the root level "Ethereum Project", do a couple of times:


	cd ..



- With the virtual environment activated, initiate the dbt project with:


	dbt init



- In snowflake to create the database that will hold DBT transformations:


	CREATE DATABASE DBT;



- After providing all the Snowflake information, test the connection:


	cd eth

	dbt debug
