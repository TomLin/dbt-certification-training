*** Transactions enriched ***


- Under the models folder, create the file transactions_enriched.sql:


select

t.hash,
t.block_number,
t.date,
t.from_address,
t.to_address,
t.value,
t.receipt_contract_address,
t.input


from {{ source('eth','transactions')}} t


- Then back in the terminal:


	dbt run --select transactions_enriched
