*** Stablecoin activity per day ***


- Links to USDT and USDC crypto listings:

	https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/tether/
	
	https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/usd-coin/


- Token tracker address in etherscan for USDT and USDC:

	https://etherscan.io/token/0xdac17f958d2ee523a2206206994597c13d831ec7
	
	https://etherscan.io/token/0xa0b86991c6218b36c1d19d4a2e9eb0ce3606eb48




- Create a new file under models stablecoin_activity_per_day.sql:


select
date,
token_address,
sum(value/1e6) as total_usd_value

from {{ source('eth', 'token_transfers')}}


where lower(token_address) in ('0xdac17f958d2ee523a2206206994597c13d831ec7', '0xa0b86991c6218b36c1d19d4a2e9eb0ce3606eb48')

group by 
date,
token_address


- Then run from the terminal:

	dbt run --select stablecoin_activity_per_day
